
# 11.11 Sale Analytics — Sprint Plan

## User Story 1: Store Owner
As a store owner, I want to see total revenue and best-selling
categories so that I can make better business decisions.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Total revenue is calculated using delivered orders only.
- Revenue by product category is displayed in a bar chart.

## User Story 2: Marketing Team
As a marketing team member, I want to identify peak shopping
hours so that I can schedule promotions effectively.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Orders are summarized by hour.
- Peak shopping hours can be identified from a chart.

## User Story 3: Delivery Partner
As a delivery partner, I want to see order counts and cancellations
by region so that I can plan delivery resources.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Order counts are summarized by region.
- Cancellation rates can be analyzed by payment method.
